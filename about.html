About.

A timeline of key milestones in my journey through computer science, research, and software engineering.

Aug 2021

Started Undergrad at SUNY Polytechnic

Began pursuing a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science with a minor in Information Systems at SUNY Polytechnic Institute in Utica, NY.

2024

Undergraduate Research (SURP)

Joined the Summer Undergraduate Research Program, building a personal knowledge management system for AI chatbot analysis. Led Agile sprints, code reviews, and presented findings in a poster session.

2024 – 2025

AI Exploration (AIX) Research

Expanded SURP work into a multi-user platform using MERN full-stack development. Authored a TiddlyWiki guide adopted in a SUNY Poly graduate course.

2024 – 2025

Coding Competitions

Won second place at the SUNY Polytechnic Coding Competition (2024) and first place at the UtiCode Coding Competition (2025).

May 2025

Graduated from SUNY Poly

Completed B.E. in Computer Science (GPA: 3.65) with Dean’s List every semester. Capstone project: fine-tuning LLMs to teach Lua programming.

Summer 2026

NYCM Insurance Internship

Advanced Business Analytics Intern. Automated data extraction with Python and pandas, built predictive models, and delivered results through Tableau dashboards.

Fall 2025 – Present

Master’s at Binghamton University

Pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science with a focus in Artificial Intelligence (GPA: 3.85). Conducting thesis research at the intersection of quantum networking and machine learning.
