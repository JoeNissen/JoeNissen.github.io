Games.

A collection of classic mini-games, playable right in the browser.

Snake

Eat to grow without running into yourself. Three speeds, solid or wrap-around walls, keyboard or swipe.

Minesweeper

Uncover cells without hitting a mine. Right-click to flag. Three difficulty levels available.

2048

Slide numbered tiles on a 4×4 grid, merging matching pairs to reach 2048. With undo and a saved game.

Tetris

Stack falling pieces to clear lines, with hold, a ghost piece, and the next pieces shown. Faster every level.

Game of Life

Conway's cellular automaton. Draw cells or load a classic pattern like the glider gun, then watch it evolve.

Color Lines

Move balls to line up five of a colour. Every move that clears nothing adds three more; don't let the board fill.

Typing Speed Test

Type a sentence or three against the clock and see your words per minute and accuracy.
