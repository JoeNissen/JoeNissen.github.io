Games.

A collection of classic mini-games, playable right in the browser.

Snake

Classic snake game. Use arrow keys or swipe to control, eat food to grow, and avoid the walls.

Minesweeper

Uncover cells without hitting a mine. Right-click to flag. Three difficulty levels available.

2048

Slide numbered tiles on a 4×4 grid, merging matching pairs to reach 2048.

Tetris

Stack and rotate falling tetrominoes to clear lines. Speed increases with each level.

Game of Life

Conway's cellular automaton. Click cells to toggle, hit play, and watch patterns evolve.

Typing Speed Test

Test your typing speed and accuracy with programming-themed sentences in monospace.
